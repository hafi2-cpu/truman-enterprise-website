Who We Are

A Volunteer-Run Charity Opening the Canal to All

We've been offering canal trips to people across the West Midlands for over 50 years — especially those with elderly, disabled, or special needs members, and individuals facing health challenges.

We are very popular with care homes, day centres, support groups, retirement clubs and associations. Families often celebrate special birthdays on one of our trips.
